COMMON QUESTIONS

360 photo to video FAQ

How do I convert a 360 photo to a video?

Choose a 2:1 equirectangular panorama, frame the opening view, add camera waypoints for each pan, tilt or zoom, preview the path, and export the result as a WebM video.

Does PanoPath upload my panorama?

No. Decoding, WebGL rendering and video encoding happen locally in your browser. Closing the page discards the photo.

How does PanoPath prevent stuttering?

In browsers with WebCodecs, each output frame is rendered at its exact timeline position. The export may take longer on slower hardware, but the intended camera timing does not change.

What image resolution should I use?

A 4096×2048 panorama is a good source for HD output. For a sharp 4K reframed video, use an 8K equirectangular source because the virtual camera sees only part of the sphere at once.

Which browsers provide the best export?

Current Chrome and Edge releases provide the preferred frame-locked WebCodecs path. Other browsers use a real-time compatibility encoder when available.
